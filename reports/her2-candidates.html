What qualifies these numbers

No design is predicted to beat trastuzumab on affinity

Trastuzumab measures KD 0.029–0.178 nM in the provided SPR table. Our regressor predicts nothing below 1 nM across all 66 novelty-passing designs; the best is 5.9 nM. That is not the model clipping — it predicts as low as 0.05 nM on its own training data. The novelty gate forces us at least three mutations away from every measured variant, into sequence space that is on average worse. S-tier (winning all three axes) is therefore unlikely; A-tier — epitope preserved plus thermostability — is the realistic target.

The scorer declined to judge, so the threshold is ours

refold_metrics.csv returned an empty pass column, not True: target-aligned RMSD is undefined when fewer than three target atoms are fixed in absolute coordinates. These designs were not approved — they were not disqualified. The ranking uses the metrics the documentation recommends in that case (min_ipae, iptm, binder_plddt) plus backbone RMSD.

The de novo track was tested and dropped

200 de novo VHH nanobodies were designed against the same epitope. Of 40 validated, zero survived: binder pLDDT ~0.92 (they fold well) but 15–51 Å from the designed pose, and AF2-IG independently returned ipTM 0.11–0.34 against a 0.5 threshold. Well-formed proteins with no interface. Adaptyv's EGFR competition agrees with the direction: optimised binders hit 18–28 %, the best de novo method 9 %.
