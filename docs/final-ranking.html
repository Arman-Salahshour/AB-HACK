Odyssey

Final ranking: 30 HER2 antibody designs

Rank 1 is the one to make first.

I · Unmet need

Strike the tumor,
spare the heart

We target HER2 in the tumor microenvironment, not the HER2 the heart relies on.

The tumor microenvironment is more acidic (around pH 6.5) than blood (7.4). A histidine gains a charge as pH falls, so a design can in principle hold on harder in the tumor. We predict this for every design; it is a prediction, not a measurement.

II · Our voyage

Pipeline

  1. Generate225 unique optimisation designs (a new CDRH3 loop on trastuzumab's framework) and 220 de novo nanobodies.
  2. Filter139 pass the novelty rules and 130 pass the pH gate; only designs with a structure refold go forward (18 parents).
  3. Cross-checkBoltz-2 interface error, then Mol-1 with no template: all 22 optimisation designs sit in trastuzumab's binding pose.
  4. RankBinding models decide the order. pH and safety break ties; they never override.
  5. TwinThe top 4 are followed by a framework twin (VH S85N + R87T) that the literature links to higher melting temperature.
III · Landfall

Results

30 designs, ranked: 22 optimisation (18 parents and 4 twins) and 8 de novo, placed last because no independent check found them credible. Nothing here is a measured result: affinity, thermostability and pH selectivity are predictions.

Binder probability and predicted KD come from models trained on measured data (trastuzumab itself scores 0.995 and about 0.2 nM). Mol-1 is an AlphaFold 3-family model run with no template; pose is how far its antibody sits from trastuzumab's crystal pose after aligning HER2. pH select. is the predicted log10 of binding at 6.5 over 7.4 (about ±0.1–0.3 noise). Loop gap is how much less confident the refold is about the new CDRH3 than about the framework (lower is better). Twin rows reuse their parent's structure numbers.

Top six at a glance

Four axes, 0–1, higher is better. Thermostability is the loop-confidence proxy and safety is a sequence proxy, not a toxicity prediction.

What is measured and what is not

QuestionWhat we haveHow far to trust it
AffinityBinder and KD models trained on 525k measured variants; Boltz-2 interface error Predictions only. Our best design is predicted about 27× weaker than trastuzumab, so we do not expect to win on affinity.
ThermostabilityFramework twins from the literature; a loop-confidence proxy; thermal MD on trastuzumab only No design was simulated, because the MD was stopped early. The twins are the real bet.
pH 6.5 versus 7.4A pKa-based prediction for every design; two-pH MD on the reference Unvalidated and noisy; the MD gap on the reference is within replica noise.
SafetySequence liabilities, hydrophobicity and charge A proxy. Trastuzumab's cardiac toxicity is target-mediated, so every design on this epitope inherits the risk.
StructureBoltz-2 (with the trastuzumab template); Mol-1 and OpenFold3 (no template) Mol-1 reproduces trastuzumab's pose and scores the designs the same, so it supports the binding mode but cannot tell good loops from bad. Hosted OpenFold3 failed on trastuzumab itself, so its result was discarded.

The simulations

Each line is one replica, drawn as far as it got (10 ps per frame). 1 means every starting contact is still present. These runs were stopped early and cover the trastuzumab reference, not our designs, so they check the method and set a noise level; they do not rank anything.

IV · The crew and the journey ahead

Next steps and who we are

  1. Measure the top designs. Binding and thermostability in the lab; every number here is still a prediction.
  2. Finish the simulations on our designs. Thermal and two-pH MD for the top design and its twin never completed.
  3. Test the pH idea directly. Compare binding at 6.5 and 7.4 for the designs predicted to prefer 6.5.
  4. Add an energy estimate (MM/GBSA on the MD frames) to the ranking; there is none yet.
