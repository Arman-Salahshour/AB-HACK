HER2 final ranking

30 designs, ranked. Rank 1 is the one to make first. 22 keep trastuzumab's framework with a new CDRH3 (18 designs plus 4 framework twins); 8 are de novo nanobodies, placed last because none passed an independent structure check. Nothing here is a measured result: affinity, thermostability and pH selectivity are predictions.

How the ranking was made

  1. Hard filters. The organisers' validator accepts all 30 (valid sequences, novel CDRH3, no unpaired cysteine). Of 225 generated optimisation designs, 139 were novel, 130 survived the pH gate, and 18 of the best 50 had a structure refold and went forward.
  2. Binding evidence ranks first. The binder model, trained on 525,000 measured variants, and the Boltz-2 interface error decide the order. ipTM is not used: it barely separates binders from non-binders here.
  3. Thermostability. The top 4 designs are each followed by a twin carrying two published framework mutations (VH S85N + R87T) reported to raise melting temperature while keeping HER2 binding. It is the only literature-backed route to beating trastuzumab on stability.
  4. pH and safety break ties, they do not override. Designs that clearly bind better at plasma pH 7.4 than at tumour pH 6.5 were removed. A sequence-liability count is a small penalty.
  5. De novo last. Independent checks found no credible binder among them, so they fill the end of the list and are made only if extra allocation is won.

Binder probability and predicted KD come from models trained on measured data (trastuzumab itself scores 0.995 and about 0.2 nM). Mol-1 is an AlphaFold 3-family model run with no template; pose is how far its Fv sits from trastuzumab's crystal pose after aligning HER2 (shown for the 22 designs checked). pH select. is the predicted log10 of binding at 6.5 over 7.4 (about ±0.1–0.3 noise). Loop gap is how much less confident the refold is about the new CDRH3 than the framework (lower is better).

Top six at a glance

Four axes, 0–1, higher is better. Thermostability here is the loop-confidence proxy, and safety is a sequence proxy (liabilities, hydrophobicity, charge), not a toxicity prediction.

What is measured and what is not

QuestionWhat we haveHow far to trust it
AffinityBinder and KD models trained on 525k measured variants; Boltz-2 interface error Predictions only. Our best design is predicted about 27× weaker than trastuzumab, so we do not expect to win on affinity.
ThermostabilityFramework twins from the literature; a loop-confidence proxy; thermal MD on trastuzumab only No design was simulated, because the MD was stopped early. The twins are the real bet.
pH 6.5 versus 7.4A pKa-based prediction for every design; two-pH MD on the reference Unvalidated and noisy; the MD gap on the reference is within replica noise.
SafetySequence liabilities, hydrophobicity and charge A proxy. Trastuzumab's cardiac toxicity is target-mediated, so every design on this epitope inherits the risk.
StructureBoltz-2 (with the trastuzumab template); Mol-1 and OpenFold3 (no template) Mol-1 reproduces trastuzumab's pose and scores the designs the same, so it supports the binding mode but cannot tell good loops from bad. Hosted OpenFold3 failed on trastuzumab itself, so its result was discarded.

The simulations

Each line is one replica, drawn as far as it got (10 ps per frame). 1 means every starting contact is still present. These runs were stopped early and cover the trastuzumab reference, not our designs, so they check the method and set a noise level; they do not rank anything.
