HER2 design pipeline

Six stages from a design spec to twenty designs Adaptyv can order. Three gates decide what survives: novelty and measured confidence are enforced before ranking; developability is only partly covered (liability penalty and framework tickets, no solubility or aggregation predictors yet). A fourth question is now scored but not yet enforced: does the design bind at tumour pH (6.5) and less at plasma pH (7.4)?

Why ranking changed away from ipTM

The Smorodina benchmark gives 91 real cognate nanobody–antigen pairs on the diagonal of a 91×91 matrix and roughly 8,100 shuffled pairings off it, scored by the same models we use. That makes the filter testable.

At the conventional 0.8 cutoff Boltz-2 admits 81.7% of known non-binders against 90.1% of real pairs: 1.10× enrichment, effectively no information. Its usable cutoff is 0.951, where it keeps under a fifth of true binders. Chai-1's 89× is a trap — it keeps 1.1% of real pairs, meaning one. Adaptyv's EGFR results put the same metrics at ~0.2 correlation with measured outcome, which is the same conclusion from real experiments rather than shuffled pairs.

What the refold check caught

The de novo VHH track was dropped on measured evidence

100 de novo VHHs were generated and the top 20 refolded. Every one failed. The nanobodies fold well on their own — binder_ptm 0.95–0.99, binder_plddt 89–94 — but Boltz-2 places them 10.9 to 55.2 Å from the designed pose. They are well-formed domains that bind somewhere else.

Two alternative explanations were ruled out by measurement. The target is not at fault: superposing each refold on the template gives chain C a self-RMSD of 0.45 Å, chain D displaced 2.0 Å, and the C572–D591 gap preserved at 15.8 Å against 16.25 Å designed — so splitting HER2 domain IV across two chains did no harm. And the metrics contradict each other, which is the decisive tell: the design with the best interface PAE (2.48) and best ipTM (0.82) sits 39 Å from its designed pose, while the best-docked design (10.9 Å) has an interface PAE of 18.2. No design is good on both, so none looks like a real binder.

One explanation was open: the first refold ran with msa_path: None on every chain, so Boltz-2 predicted single-sequence, and 0/20 may have overstated how bad the designs were. AF2-IG was the control, because it starts from the designed coordinates instead of rediscovering the pose. It has now run on 40 designs (two seeds, 20 each): ipTM 0.09–0.34 and interface PAE 19–27 Å for every one, while binder pLDDT stays 0.89–0.93. Two independent architectures agree, so the single-sequence caveat no longer rescues the track. BoltzGen designs scored weakly too (best ipTM 0.32 on its own scores, PAE 8.8–18 Å, and 0.46 / PAE 16 Å for the best under AF2-IG). A refold with a full MSA on the same designs was not run, so the Boltz-2 numbers alone still carry that caveat.

Root cause at the level of the weights: de novo ran on antibody-2026-10-30, the general antibody checkpoint, which knows nothing about HER2. Both HER2-finetuned checkpoints only redesign CDRH3 on a fixed framework, so the de novo track could not use the one asset actually trained on this target.

pH selectivity: bind at tumour pH, less at plasma pH

Trastuzumab's known class toxicity is cardiac, and it is target-mediated, so any design that keeps the domain IV epitope inherits it. The one lever in this pipeline aimed at that is conditional binding: stronger at tumour pH (6.5) than at plasma pH (7.4). Nothing below is measured; the judged assay is not documented as pH-controlled, so it may not reward this.

StageWhat it doesState
ph_interface.py Maps paratope residues that touch or sit within 7 Å of a HER2 carboxylate (Glu558, Asp560, Asp570, Asp596, Glu598) done
phswitch.py 300 histidine-switch candidates with a novel CDRH3 (the novelty gate needs one) and 1–5 His at the contact core and paratope done
phscore.py Propka on the complex and the free Fv; predicted log10 K(6.5) − log10 K(7.4) summed over every titratable group. Trastuzumab control: 0.00 done
run_ph_md.py Fv + HER2 complex MD at each pH; each His protonated with probability set by its bound-state pKa; interface contact retention 3 control runs, batch stopped
abhack.ranking --ph-scores and --ph-md add two optional composite terms (weights 1.0 and 0.5); absent columns are skipped wired, not applied to the submission

What the scoring found. Among the 20 submitted designs, 3 reach +0.3 (about 2×), 4 reach +0.1, and 14 are at or above zero. The signal in the submitted designs comes from CDRH3 acids shifting up, not from histidines. Of the 300 histidine candidates, 2 reach +0.3 and 40 are at or above zero; the best is +0.56 (about 3.6×). Interface histidines usually lose protonation on binding, so a real switch needs one whose pKa an acid partner pushes toward 7. The de novo design that scores highest has a likely wrong pose (AF2-IG ipTM 0.32), so its score does not count.

Proposed rule, not yet applied. Remove designs with a negative selectivity (they bind better at high pH), which drops 6 of the current 20. Give pH credit only when propka reaches +0.3 and the MD contact-retention gap exceeds twice the control's replica spread in the same direction. A gate at +0.3 alone would leave 3 designs and could not fill 20 slots. The ranking order stays: measured-data binding models first, then framework stability, then pH, then developability.

Caveats. Propka is off by about one pKa unit and the candidates are threaded onto the 1N8Z crystal, not refolded. A 1 ns MD at fixed protonation states is a sanity check, not evidence. HER2's own histidines are protonated at 6.5 and neutral at 7.4, which adds a real antigen-side difference to every comparison.

Open gaps

  • Thermostability is a third of the result and has only proxies: two literature framework mutations (S85N+R87T, which also creates an NLT glycosylation sequon, flagged and not filtered) and a CDRH3 liability penalty. Aggrescan3D, SoluProt, NetSolP and TAP-style developability checks are not installed. The thermal MD screen passed a plumbing test on one trastuzumab structure (0.05 ns) but has no baseline or shortlist results. NetMHCIIpan on the changed CDRH3 is the only immunogenicity check that would discriminate between these designs; humanness scores barely would, since the framework is shared.
  • epitope_fraction discriminates as a measurement (37 of 100 de novo designs below 1.0) but did not predict docking success on the refolded set, so it measures the judged axis and is not a binding predictor.
  • The calibration negatives are computational shuffled pairings, not measured non-binders, so every false-positive rate is a lower bound on what an optimised design would see.
  • Boltz-2 numbers on the optimisation track partly reflect a templated crystal complex supplying the answer. OpenFold3 / Protenix refolds would check this.
